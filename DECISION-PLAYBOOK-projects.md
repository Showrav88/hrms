## Binders (hard limits)

1. **One job anchor:** Moderation targets come first unless manager **reduces** them in writing/chat.
2. **No triple load:** Not moderation + 4h client + “whenever” nights without **Y hrs/week** cap.
3. **POC before production:** Small deliverable + date. No open-ended client owner.
4. **No private career on office PC:** Nafiz, salary, this doc, long strategy chats → **personal phone / home machine only**.
5. **No room confessions:** Don’t say hate moderation, desperate for Nafiz, or unlimited yes.
6. **Recap always:** After any meeting, 4–6 lines — scope, hours, who approves.

## Tonight / meetings — script (practice aloud)

**Open:**  
> I want to understand client, scope, timeline, and how this fits moderation — then I’ll say what I can deliver.

**If WFH + 4h + moderation:**  
> I’m interested. I need moderation **adjusted** or **Y hrs/week** on project recorded — otherwise I can only commit to a **POC by [date]**.

**If vague “you lead”:**  
> What does lead mean — build, client calls, support? Who signs off?

**Close:**  
> I’ll wait for your confirm on scope and hours, then start.

## “Not feeling well” message — if they ask what happened

**You are not unsafe.** You paused when load had **no clear scope**. Use **one** short story everywhere — no Nafiz, no trust rant, no “I let you down” loop.

| Situation | Say this (practice) |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **They ask directly** | Bhai, I wasn’t feeling well for a bit, and I saw client work was getting heavy **without clear scope** — I didn’t want to miss moderation or deliver badly. I’m better now and still interested. |
| **Before tonight / Friday** | Sorry if my last message sounded like I’m stepping back fully — it was health + workload clarity. I want to align on **scope and hours**, then I can commit to a **POC**. |
| **If they feel let down** | I understand the urgency — that’s why I’m here. I need a **defined deliverable and time cap** so I don’t disappear again on moderation. |
| **If they push guilt (“we counted on you”)** | I don’t want the client to lose either. I can do **[POC by date]** within **[Y hrs/week]** if we agree that in writing. |
| **Do not say** | Hate moderation, unlimited yes, Nafiz/salary, “I don’t trust you,” long apology paragraphs. |

**Repeat rule:** Same 2–3 sentences every time. Human nature will ask once or twice — **consistent** answer = reliable, not flaky.
